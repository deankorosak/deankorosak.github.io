What this simulates

Recruitment layer. N cells carry binary states σi ∈ {0, 1}. Each polls a fixed set of k partners drawn once at random (a quenched k-out graph) and owns a glucose midpoint Gc,i = Gc + ξi, ξi ~ 𝒩(0, σG²). On every sweep each cell is redrawn with probability pu and takes

σi ← Bernoulli[ α mi + (1 − α) fi(G) ],  fi(G) = 1 / (1 + e−(G − Gc,i)/dG)

where mi = 1 when a strict majority of its partners is recruited. Averaged over a locally tree-like graph this is the mean-field gradient flow φ̇ = α[P(φ; k) − φ] + (1 − α)[f(G) − φ] = −V′(φ), whose cusp at αc = 1/P′(½; k) opens a bistable tongue and a first-order, hysteretic switch; the Bernoulli sampling restores the demographic noise D ∝ 1/N, and ξi is the random field that rounds and pins the transition.

Emission layer. A frozen ON state has no correlations, so each cell is also assigned once to one of m regions and emits the Ca2+ proxy

yi(t) = σi(t) [ w0 z0(t) + wr zr(i)(t) ] + s ηi(t)

with z0 a common-drive fluctuation, zr a regional one (all unit-variance AR(1) with coefficient ϱ), and ηi private white noise. Recruited cells of one region correlate at ρin, across regions at ρout, and any pair with a resting cell at 0, so the pairwise-correlation distribution turns bimodal on the upper branch with strong-component weight w ≈ φ² Σr qr², and exactly m eigenvalues detach from the Marčenko–Pastur bulk.

Pipeline. The signals are treated like recordings: Pearson correlation matrices in sliding windows of W sweeps, the two-component Gaussian-mixture order parameter Δ = |2w − 1|, eigenvalues against the Marčenko–Pastur law at Q = W/N, the contribution matrix of the eigenvalues above λ+ whose strongest edges define the functional cluster S(t), its overlap U(t, τ) with the plateau windows, and the consecutive-window ARI of an average-linkage clustering. A second, quasi-static experiment steps glucose up and down with the configuration carried over, and reads φ and Δ at each level against the deterministic branches.

Glucose protocol (a)

Five segments of equal length: baseline at Gbase, a linear ramp to Gpeak, a plateau, the ramp back, baseline. The ramps are not quasi-static, so the jumps in (b) lag the deterministic folds.

Emergence of synchronization (b)

φ (recruited fraction)Δ = |2w − 1| (GMM)

The recruited fraction switches from 0 to 1 within a few tens of sweeps on the up-ramp and back on the down-ramp. Δ, read from the emitted signals alone, rises with it to the plateau predicted by the emission closure, Δ ≈ 1 − 2φ² Σr qr².

Hysteresis in recruitment (c)

mean-field branchesup (sim)down (sim)

Quasi-static loop: glucose stepped up and then down with the configuration carried over, relaxed and averaged at each level, against the fixed points of the mean-field equation iterated from φ = 0 and from φ = 1. The finite-N loop is narrower than the deterministic one because demographic noise carries the system over the barrier before the fold (Kramers escape).

Hysteresis in the correlation order parameter (d)

up (sim)down (sim)

The same loop read entirely from the correlations: Δ switches between ≈ 0 and its plateau value at the glucose levels of (c), so the pipeline recovers the recruitment transition and its hysteresis without access to the states σi.

Off-diagonal correlations (log scale). Basal: one mode at zero of width ≈ W−1/2, the sampling noise of uncorrelated series. Plateau: bimodal, with resting and cross-region pairs near ρout and within-region pairs near ρin; the fitted two-component mixture and its components are overlaid.

Eigenvalue spectrum (f)

basal windowsplateau windowsMarčenko–Pasturλ+

Pooled eigenvalues of the basal and plateau windows against the Marčenko–Pastur law at Q = W/N. At baseline all lie inside the bulk; on the plateau the bulk of private noise remains and m eigenvalues, one per coherent mode, sit far beyond λ+ (ticks above the axis mark them).

Functional-cluster overlap with plateau (g)

U(t, τ) mean over τ± s.d. over τ

S(t) is the set of cells on the strongest edges of the contribution matrix built from the eigenvalues above λ+; U is its recall against each plateau window τ. Zero at baseline (no eigenvalue exceeds λ+, S is empty), it jumps at the up-transition and holds through the plateau. With m equally coherent regions the strongest edges are drawn from ~10³ nearly equivalent within-region pairs, so U saturates below unity.

Cluster stability (h)

consecutive-window ARIrolling mean (9 windows)

Adjusted Rand index between the average-linkage clusterings (dij = 1 − |ρij|) of consecutive windows. Low at baseline, near unity on the plateau where the same cells make up the same modes window after window, decreasing on deactivation.
